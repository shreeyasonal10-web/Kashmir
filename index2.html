About Kashmir

Kashmir is known as the “Paradise on Earth” because of its beautiful mountains, lakes, and valleys. 🏔️ It is famous for places like Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Dal Lake. 🌸 Kashmir is also known for its scenic beauty, traditional handicrafts, and delicious food. ❤️

Srinagar:

Srinagar is a beautiful city in Jammu and Kashmir, known for its scenic beauty. 🏔️ It is famous for Dal Lake, Shikara rides, and houseboats. 🚤

Gulmarg:

Gulmarg is a beautiful hill station in Jammu and Kashmir, famous for its snowy mountains. 🏔️ It is popular for skiing and snowboarding. ❄️

Pahalgam:

Pahalgam is a beautiful hill station in Kashmir, surrounded by green valleys and snow-capped mountains. It is popular for trekking and sightseeing.

Sonamarg:

Sonamarg is a beautiful hill station in Kashmir, famous for its snow-covered mountains. It is known as the “Meadow of Gold”. 🏔️✨

Yusmarg:

Yusmarg is a peaceful hill station in Kashmir, surrounded by beautiful green meadows and forests. 🌲 It is perfect for nature lovers.

Doodhpathri:

Doodhpathri is a beautiful meadow in Kashmir, surrounded by lush green hills and forests. 🌿 It is famous for its peaceful atmosphere and natural beauty.
